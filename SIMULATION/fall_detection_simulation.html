How the Fall-o-Up Algorithm Mitigates False Positives

1. Precondition (Free-fall & Impact)

A simple accelerometer-only trigger (like detecting a >3G force) fires false alarms on daily activities like clapping hands, typing heavily, or jumping. Fall-o-Up checks for a preceding weightless phase (SMV < 0.5g for at least 120ms) before the impact spike, filtering out basic impacts.

2. Post-Impact Stillness (Gate 1)

If a person drops their arm quickly or stumbles but stands back up instantly (active recovery), no emergency alert is required. The device measures mathematical variance of the three-axis acceleration over a 2.0-second window. If variance exceeds 0.20g, movement is ongoing, causing the trigger to self-reset.

3. Gyroscopic Tumbling (Gate 2)

Drops on tables (desk slams) create vertical impact acceleration but cause zero tumbling rotations. A real human fall is unstable and introduces multi-axis rotation. The firmware requires angular roll/pitch rates from the gyroscope to exceed 120°/s within the collision window.

4. 3D Posture Orientation (Gate 3)

A genuine fall alters the user's posture from vertical to horizontal. The device stores pre-fall baseline gravity direction and calculates the angle difference against the post-impact stillness gravity direction. A posture tilt of > 72° confirms the user is lying flat.

5. Multi-Modal Fusion Score

To secure a 99.8% detection reliability, the backend combines the IoT sensor flags with real-time Computer Vision camera frames, audio distress monitoring, and patient vitals. A weighted sum score is calculated. If the camera is blinded or offline, the wearable has logic to override and alert caretakers directly.
